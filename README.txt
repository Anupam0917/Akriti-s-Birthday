AKRITI SINGH — BIRTHDAY WEBSITE ♡

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Keep index.html and akriti.jpeg in the same folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open it in your browser.

PHOTO
Akriti's uploaded photo is included as akriti.jpeg and displayed in the scrapbook-style photo section. Keep the image beside index.html when deploying the website.

DESIGN
- Scrapbook-style photo frame with hearts, sparkles, and bow stickers.
- Removed Birthday Cake, Our Memories, and Little Surprises boxes/sections.
- Keeps the dreamy pink-purple theme and personalized birthday letter.
- Mobile-friendly and static: no backend or installation needed.

You can edit index.html in VS Code.
